## 3. Competition creation

1. Sign in as an active Venue Owner with Premium trial/subscription.
2. Open Competition Manager.
3. Create:
   - one League,
   - one Knockout,
   - one Group → Knockout competition.
4. Confirm all begin as private DRAFT.
5. Confirm expired/no-entitlement owner cannot create a new competition.
6. Confirm another venue owner cannot open/manage this venue's competition.

## 4. Registration and capacity

1. Open registration.
2. Confirm the competition becomes publicly discoverable.
3. As a team manager, apply one managed team.
4. Confirm a non-manager cannot register that team.
5. Accept the application as venue owner.
6. Fill accepted teams to maxTeams.
7. Confirm another acceptance returns COMPETITION_FULL.
8. Test direct owner team invitation and manager response.
9. Close registration and confirm new registration changes are blocked once fixtures are generated.

## 5. League

1. Close registration with at least 3 accepted teams.
2. Generate fixtures.
3. Confirm each pair appears exactly once.
4. Schedule each match.
5. Enter results.
6. Confirm standings update after every result.
7. Confirm points, W/D/L, GF/GA/GD and ordering are deterministic.
8. Correct one completed score:
   - without reason → rejected;
   - with reason → accepted and standings recompute.
9. Confirm competition cannot complete while any required fixture lacks a result.
10. Complete it after all fixtures have results.

## 6. Knockout

1. Use 5 accepted teams to exercise non-power-of-two byes.
2. Generate knockout fixtures.
3. Confirm bracket has no fake playable bye matches.
4. Confirm byes are distributed through seeded positions and later-round links are valid.
5. Schedule and score each playable match.
6. Confirm a draw is rejected.
7. Confirm each winner advances to the correct next-round side.
8. Correct a winner:
   - downstream match started/completed → blocked;
   - downstream merely scheduled → requires impact confirmation.
9. Complete final and confirm championTeamId.

## 7. Group → Knockout

1. Create 4+ accepted teams with at least 2 groups.
2. Generate group fixtures.
3. Confirm deterministic group assignment.
4. Enter every group result.
5. Attempt GENERATE_KNOCKOUT before groups finish → rejected.
6. Attempt COMPLETE after groups finish but before knockout generation → rejected with KNOCKOUT_INCOMPLETE.
7. Generate knockout.
8. Confirm qualifiers match configured qualifiersPerGroup and group standings.
9. Schedule/score knockout fixtures.
10. Confirm champion.
11. Correct a group result after knockout snapshot:
   - without impact confirmation when required → rejected;
   - before knockout play begins + confirmation → bracket recalculates;
   - after knockout play materially begins → safeguarded according to downstream state.

## 8. Booking-calendar conflict — match first

1. Schedule a competition match for Area A at a future interval.
2. Open public availability for that date.
3. Confirm the overlapping slot is absent/non-bookable.
4. Attempt online booking for the exact interval via API/UI.
5. Confirm booking is rejected as unavailable.
6. Attempt owner manual booking/block over it.
7. Confirm conflict is rejected.

## 9. Booking-calendar conflict — booking first

1. Create a confirmed/manual booking for Area A.
2. Attempt to schedule a competition match over the booking.
3. Confirm SLOT_UNAVAILABLE.
4. Repeat with an owner block.
5. Repeat with another scheduled competition match.
6. Confirm all use the same conflict boundary.
